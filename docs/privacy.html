4. How bank data is accessed (Enable Banking)

  • Connecting a bank is optional. The rest of the app works without it.
  • When you connect, you are redirected to your bank (e.g. Swedbank Latvia) to log in and approve access. The app never sees or stores your bank login credentials.
  • Access is read-only (account information only): accounts, balances and transactions. The app cannot initiate payments or move money.
  • Access is limited to the accounts and the period you approve at your bank. Consent expires after the period set by the bank / regulation (typically up to 180 days) and can be renewed by you.
  • Enable Banking acts as a licensed account information service provider and processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Bank proxy (Cloudflare Worker)

  • Enable Banking requires every request to be signed with the application’s private key. To keep that key out of the app, the phone talks to a small proxy run by the developer on Cloudflare Workers. The proxy holds the key, signs the request and forwards it to Enable Banking; the response is passed straight back to the phone.
  • The proxy is stateless: it does not store bank data, session data or request/response bodies, and does not log them. Cloudflare may keep its own platform-level technical logs and metadata (for example IP address and time of a request) under Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Cloudflare acts as a processor (hosting provider) for this proxy.
  • Only the app holding the proxy’s access key can use it.
  • Where a bank requires it, your device’s IP address is forwarded to Enable Banking/the bank as the “PSU IP address” when you actively refresh.

When data is fetched

  • Right after you connect, and whenever you tap Sync now.
  • Automatically at most once every 6 hours, and only when you open the Money section of the app (there is no background job). The first sync covers up to the last 90 days of transactions; later syncs fetch recent days only.

5. Where data is stored

  • All app data, including bank data fetched through Enable Banking, is stored in a local database inside the app’s private storage on your phone. There is no LifeAPI cloud account or LifeAPI server that stores your data (the bank proxy described in section 4 only passes data through).
  • Connection details (proxy address and access key, Enable Banking session id and consent end date) are kept in the app’s private settings storage on the phone, unencrypted. Bank login credentials are never stored.
  • The app database is not encrypted by the app itself; it relies on Android’s app sandbox and the device encryption provided by your phone. An optional PIN / fingerprint lock restricts opening the app, but does not encrypt the data.
  • Backups you export from Settings are saved as unencrypted JSON files to a location you choose. You are responsible for where you keep them.
  • Android system backup: the app allows Android’s automatic backup. If backup is enabled on your device, Android may copy app data to your Google account under Google’s terms. [TO CONFIRM: keep or disable Android auto-backup before connecting a bank]

6. Other services the app can contact

Only when you use the related feature:

  • Enable Banking and, through it, your bank — bank connection (section 4), via the developer’s Cloudflare Workers proxy.
  • Open Food Facts — product lookup by barcode or name. The barcode or search text is sent; no personal data.
  • FatSecret (optional, via a proxy server you configure yourself) — food search and, if you sign in, reading your FatSecret food diary.

Bank data is not sent to any of these food services. The “Intelligence” insights in the app are computed on the phone; no data is sent to AI providers.

7. Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed only to provide the app’s features to you: showing your accounts, balances and transactions, categorising spending, budgets and statistics. Bank data is accessed only on the basis of your explicit consent given at your bank, which you can withdraw at any time. Data is not sold, shared for marketing, or used for profiling or credit decisions.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual records, use Settings → Security & data → Erase all data, or uninstall the app (which removes its local database).
  • Disconnecting a bank stops further access by the app. You can also revoke consent at your bank or with Enable Banking.
  • Data already held by your bank or by Enable Banking is retained under their own policies.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the data is stored on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (view, edit, export to JSON, erase). For anything else, contact [contact e-mail]. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Latvia: the Data State Inspectorate, dvi.gov.lv).

10. Security

The app relies on Android’s app sandbox, the device lock and encryption, HTTPS connections to external services, and the optional in-app PIN / fingerprint lock. No system is completely secure; protect your phone with a screen lock and keep it updated.
